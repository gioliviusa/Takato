"The Art of Umami"

The Chef's Story

Executive Chef Taek "Taka" Lee brings a lifetime of culinary mastery to the Conrad Fort Lauderdale Beach. Born in Korea and driven by a fascination with Japanese cuisine, Chef Lee's journey spans over 26 years of perfecting his craft.

His distinguished career includes tenure at world-renowned establishments such as ZUMA, Makoto, and Monkitail. At TAKATO, he masterfully blends the soul of his Korean ancestry with the precise, delicate artistry of Japanese cuisine.

The result is a decadent love letter to South Florida — a menu that is modern, vibrant, and deeply rooted in tradition, served in a space that mirrors the food: sharp, elegant, and unexpectedly dynamic.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the dress code?

We recommend Business Casual attire for all guests visiting TAKATO.

Do I need a reservation?

Reservations are highly recommended and can be made via OpenTable or by calling us directly at (954) 414-5160.

Can you accommodate large parties?

Yes, for parties larger than 6, please contact our host directly at host@takatorestaurant.com to arrange your dining experience.

Where are you located?

We are located inside the Conrad Fort Lauderdale Beach at 551 N Fort Lauderdale Beach Blvd, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33304.

Is there a cancellation policy?

Yes, for Omakase and large party reservations, a cancellation fee may apply if not cancelled within 24 hours. Please refer to your reservation confirmation for details.
